# Worksheet 3: The V.A.L.U.E. Edit

| Check | Question | Ready / Revise / Unknown |
|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Can the intended reader recognize the subject in the opening? | |
| **Attention** | Does the rehook add meaning, and does the body keep the promise? | |
| **Legitimacy** | Are claims supported, examples labeled, limits proportionate? | |
| **Usefulness** | Can the reader make a better decision or complete a task? | |
| **Engagement-to-Action** | Is the next step clear, appropriate and working? | |

*Don't add up the marks into a pretend virality score. Fix every "unknown" fact and every broken destination before you publish.*

## The V.I.R.A.L. → L.E.A.D. one-pager

| Inside the post (V.I.R.A.L.) | | After the post (L.E.A.D.) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Voice** | Sound like a human who understands the work | **Lead** | Create a reason to continue the relationship |
| **Intrigue** | Earn the next line (hook + rehook) | **Engagement** | Start a real exchange, and reply to substance |
| **Relevance** | Make the right reader see themselves | **Alignment** | Check whether your help fits |
| **Authority** | Experience + evidence + insight | **Discovery** | Move the right people into a business conversation |
| **Lesson** | Leave them able to act | | |

## The pre-publication checklist

- ☐ One recognizable reader and one primary job.
- ☐ The opening promises only what the body delivers.
- ☐ Every example is real, attributed or clearly labeled illustrative.
- ☐ Numbers include enough context to read them honestly.
- ☐ Client information and quotes have permission.
- ☐ The lesson includes a useful action or decision rule.
- ☐ The CTA matches the reader's likely task and your capacity.
- ☐ The resource or commercial destination works on mobile.
- ☐ No bracketed template instructions remain.

</section>

# The Common Mistakes Repair Sheet

| Mistake | Why it hurts | First repair |
|---|---|---|
| Broad hooks unrelated to your offer | Attention with little buyer relevance | Rewrite around a recognizable buyer situation |
| Unsupported result claims | Weakens credibility; misleads | Add evidence, qualify or remove |
| Every post asks for a call | Asks for more effort than the post earned | Match the CTA rung to awareness |
| Resource requests counted as leads | Free-item demand inflates the pipeline | Separate requests, conversations and opportunities |
| Engagement-bait CTAs ("Agree?") | Noisy signal; works against relevance | Ask for experience or application instead |
| Blind DM sequences after comments | Ignores context and boundaries | Reply to the actual signal |
| A confusing profile | Readers can't connect the post to a service | Align audience, problem, evidence and next step |
| Constantly changing strategy | Results become unreadable | Hold one focus for a full cycle |
| Copying first-person templates | Fiction presented as personal evidence | Use real history or a transparent demonstration |
| Measuring only impressions | Exposure mistaken for progress | Track fit and commercial progression |
| Publishing beyond capacity | Research, replies and delivery suffer | Post less; protect quality |
| Engagement pods / manufactured comments | Inauthentic, and LinkedIn says it acts against it | Distribute through real relevance and collaboration |

</section>
