# Chapter 22: 50 Hook Templates

<p class="lede">Structures for an idea you can support. Replace the brackets with a real audience, situation or fact.</p>

A hook is ready only when the body can keep its promise. The examples show wording, not verified results. First-person versions require your own true experience.

<div class="table-caption">Problem hooks</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Your [process] may be waiting for [missing decision]. | "Your proposal may be waiting for an owner, not another revision." |
| 02 | If [symptom] keeps happening, check [specific point]. | "If onboarding keeps slipping, check the handoff before kickoff." |
| 03 | [Role], where does [work] stop when you're unavailable? | "Agency founders: where do proposals stop when you're on vacation?" |
| 04 | The expensive part of [problem] may be [overlooked consequence]. | "The expensive part of a vague brief is the decisions it postpones." |
| 05 | Before fixing [visible symptom], check [upstream factor]. | "Before fixing follow-up, check what the buyer agreed to do next." |
| 06 | Most [audience] don't have a [assumed problem]. They have a [real problem]. | "Most freelancers don't have an income problem. They have a pipeline problem." *(from my own feed)* |

<div class="table-caption">Curiosity hooks</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 07 | Two [similar things]. One important difference: [factor]. | "Two busy pipelines. One important difference: who owns the next step." |
| 08 | The [ordinary artifact] reveals more than [obvious metric]. | "A proposal recap reveals more than the stage label in your CRM." |
| 09 | What happens between [event A] and [event B]? | "What happens between a demo request and the first useful reply?" |
| 10 | There's a missing question in [common process]. | "There's a missing question in most content briefs: who should act?" |
| 11 | I changed my view on [topic] after [real observation]. | "I changed my view on templates after watching one hide an unclear decision." |
| 12 | There are 2 types of [role] right now. (Only one [desired outcome].) | "There are 2 types of AI consultants right now. Only one gets businesses interested." *(from my own feed)* |

<div class="table-caption">Contrarian hooks</div>

| 15 | [Tool] can't decide [human question]. | "A CRM can't decide what your team means by 'qualified.'" |
| 16 | The case against [practice] when [situation]. | "The case against daily posting when you have no time to answer buyers." |
| 17 | [Good thing] becomes a problem when [condition]. | "Customization becomes a problem when nobody can approve the exceptions." |
| 18 | We chase [vanity metric] as if it were [goal], then wonder [consequence]. | "We chase reach as if it were the finish line, then wonder where the revenue went." *(from my own feed)* |

<div class="table-caption">Resource and offer hooks</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 48 | I made [resource] for [reader in a situation]. | "I made a handoff worksheet for founders who still approve every proposal." |
| 49 | If [problem] is a priority this quarter, here's [clear offer]. | "If onboarding delays are a priority this quarter, here's what a process review covers." |
| 50 | Who [service] is for, and what it requires. | "Who a content-to-conversation review is for, and what it asks of you." |

**Use the library deliberately.** Choose five hooks for the *same* idea, not five ideas for a hook you like. Remove any that misrepresent the evidence. Record which opening helped readers understand the topic. Reach alone can't tell you why a post worked. (All 50 are pre-loaded in the Content Engine Tracker's Hook Bank.)

# Chapter 23: 25 Rehook Templates

<p class="lede">Choose the rehook by what the opening still needs: context, mechanism, stakes, a promise or honest tension.</p>

<div class="table-caption">Add context</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | This matters most when [specific circumstance]. | "This matters most when the founder is still the final approver." |
| 02 | The issue starts after [event], not at [assumed stage]. | "The issue starts after the call, when the recommendation moves inside the buyer's team." |
| 03 | For [reader], that creates [consequence]. | "For an account lead, that creates a queue they can't clear alone." |
| 04 | Here's the situation I mean: [concrete scene]. | "Here's what I mean: three people agree, and none of them owns the next action." |
| 05 | This is about [bounded case], not every [broad category]. | "This is about repeat approvals, not every founder decision." |

<div class="table-caption">Reveal a mechanism</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 06 | Because [cause] happens before [visible symptom]. | "Because the missing decision happens before the document is written." |
| 07 | The tool records [thing]; it doesn't resolve [decision]. | "The CRM records the stage. It doesn't decide who moves it." |
| 08 | Every time [event], someone has to [hidden work]. | "Every time scope changes, someone has to reconstruct the original agreement." |
| 09 | What looks like [A] may be [B]. | "What looks like a confidence problem may be missing approval boundaries." |
| 10 | The missing link is [relationship]. | "The missing link is how a resource request becomes a useful conversation." |

<div class="table-caption">Establish stakes</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | The cost shows up as [observable effect]. | "The cost shows up as repeated revisions and missed review dates." |
| 12 | That leaves [person] unable to [important action]. | "That leaves the account lead unable to commit to a start date." |
| 13 | You can improve [metric] while [business problem] stays the same. | "You can grow comment volume while audience fit stays exactly the same." |
| 14 | The risk is making [decision] with [missing information]. | "The risk is hiring before you know which work actually needs an owner." |
| 15 | Before you add [effort], check [constraint]. | "Before you add another post, check where interested readers go next." |

<div class="table-caption">Make a useful promise</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 16 | Here are [number] checks you can use on [real object]. | "Here are three checks for your next proposal." |
| 17 | You can test this by [small action]. | "You can test this by tracing one stalled deal backward." |
| 18 | The goal is to tell [A] apart from [B]. | "The goal is to tell a price concern apart from an unclear approval path." |
| 19 | Start with [small unit], then decide whether to expand. | "Start with one handoff, then decide whether the whole workflow needs work." |
| 20 | By the end, you'll be able to [bounded task]. | "By the end, you'll be able to name the next unresolved decision." |

<div class="table-caption">Add evidence or honest tension</div>

| # | Template | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | The surprising part was [verified observation]. | "The surprising part was how little the template itself needed to change." |
| 22 | My earlier assumption was [belief]. The evidence suggested [revision]. | Only when the evidence really supports the change. |
| 23 | There's one exception worth keeping: [condition]. | "One exception worth keeping: some buyers already know exactly what they need." |
| 24 | Here's what this example shows, and what it doesn't. | Useful before a small-sample result or demonstration. |
| 25 | The practical decision is [choice], not [abstract debate]. | "The practical decision is which requests need review, not whether founders should delegate." |

**Exercise:** Pair five hooks from Chapter 22 with rehooks from *different* categories. Reject any pair that just repeats itself. Your final opening should make the topic, the stakes and the direction clearer, in less space.
