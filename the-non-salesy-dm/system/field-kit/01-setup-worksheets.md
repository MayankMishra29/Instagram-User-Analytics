# Worksheet 3: The Send Check + Pause Test

**Before any message:**

- ☐ Correct person, current role.
- ☐ The observation is accurate and actually relevant.
- ☐ No unsupported claim about their problem or results.
- ☐ My reason for speaking is clear and commercially honest.
- ☐ One primary trust job, one main question or request.
- ☐ Unnecessary praise, jargon and padding removed.
- ☐ Any link is relevant, working and genuinely included.
- ☐ The relationship supports this message; no stop instruction.
- ☐ **Screenshot Test:** I'd be comfortable if they posted this publicly.

**The Pause Test:** Am I sending this because it helps the buyer, or because I'm uncomfortable with the silence?

- ☐ Not trying to rush a gate
- ☐ Not relieving my own uncertainty
- ☐ Not adding "value" out of fear they won't respond
- ☐ Not asking for a call because I don't know what else to say
- ☐ Following the buyer's signal, not my sequence
- ☐ Not answering an objection they haven't raised
- ☐ Not creating urgency that doesn't exist

*If a factual or permission check fails, don't send. Better wording doesn't fix a false premise. And if they asked you a direct question, answer it.*
